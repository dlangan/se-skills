## Notes & Caveats

- **Platform Security 50% off** requires L4 approval — cannot be applied without VP sign-off
- **ITSM + Slack Bundle** — both SKUs MUST be quoted together on a single submission
- **Spiff (product) 65% off** — only valid for closed-lost on budget OR AF Sales without Spiff; upgrade != repricing
- **Veeva Compete** — minimum $25K deal size to qualify for cash bonus
- **Tableau SPIFF updated mid-quarter** — Tableau Next only eligible May 13–28; all Tableau eligible May 29–Jul 24
- **Executive Sponsor's Agentforce ASA** — SE ELIGIBLE; max team of 3 additional members per account
- **Voice Actions & Shopper Agent** — SE ELIGIBLE; payout based on consumption actions, not just closed deal
- **Most customer promos expire July 31, 2026** — except Free Voice Minutes (June 30)
- **All SPIFFs require gate clearance** — 70% Readiness + 90% NNAOV Commit
- **Never stack promos without verification** — check #promoforce before combining codes
- **"Upgrade != Repricing"** is the core rule. Changing editions (EE→UE) is always a valid upgrade motion.
